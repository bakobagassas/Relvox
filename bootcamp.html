Bootcamp 2026

Art, Tech and Service: Imagining an Inclusive Future

A 7-day immersive experience for young leaders ready to build skills, spark ideas, and create real change in their communities.

Apply Now

What You'll Experience

🧭

Leadership Skills

Discover your leadership style, build confidence, and learn to inspire others through action.

💻

Digital Skills

Explore AI tools, digital content creation, and responsible technology use for social impact.

🎙️

Public Speaking

Master voice, posture, storytelling, and persuasive communication in front of an audience.

🫀

Emotional Intelligence

Develop self-awareness, emotional regulation, resilience, and mindfulness practices.

🤝

Community Service

Partner with local organizations and experience the power of giving back to your community.

🌱

Social Impact Project

Design and present a real project that addresses a social issue using art, tech, or community action.

The Final Challenge

Throughout the week, teams design a project using art, technology, media or community action to address a social issue and propose practical solutions. On the final day, every team presents their work to a panel of judges and the full community.

Possible Project Formats

  • Video campaign
  • Social media campaign
  • Public speaking performance
  • Poster campaign
  • Community action project
  • Artistic performance
